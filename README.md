I'm a computational biomedicine student studying research bioinformatics, biomedical informatics and computational biology. I am involved in a wide array of omics (pharmacogenomics, transcriptomics, metabolomics), clinical psychopathology, neurobiology, and psychiatric medicine. I have participated in and coauthored federally sponsored research; developing computational models to analyze metatranscriptomic data, predictive analytics in pharmacogenomics, cladistics, phylogeny and plant taxonomy, and identifying responsive metabolic composites answering ecological growth of the endangered and largest plant species in the world.

Bioinformatics Consultant for U.S. Botanic Garden.  
Developer for [@ProjectKorra](https://github.com/ProjectKorra).
